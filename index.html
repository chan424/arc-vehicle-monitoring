Getting dependable counts

  • Camera. A steady camera looking down on the road from a footbridge, building or pole gives the best result. Avoid hand-held video and views where vehicles hide one another.
  • Counting line. Put it across free-flowing traffic, not at a stop line or junction mouth. Each vehicle is counted once when its centre crosses the line.
  • Classes. The Indian traffic detector recognises two-wheelers, auto-rickshaws and goods three-wheelers, cars (hatchback, sedan, SUV, MUV, van), buses, mini-buses, tempo travellers, LCVs, trucks and bicycles. The car and bus sub-types are kept in the CSV export. It has no tractor class; add tractors by hand with the + button on the tile.
  • Check. No video counter is exact. Compare a few minutes against a manual count and correct any mistakes in the Counted vehicles strip before you use the totals.
